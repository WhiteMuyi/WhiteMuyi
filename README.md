# Hi, I’m Shuhan Yang

I’m a PhD candidate at the University of Georgia, where I study construction and traffic safety. When I read an accident report, I try to picture the scene from the operator’s seat. Could they see the worker beside the machine? Could they tell how close they were?

Those questions also shape what I build. In one project, we tested how well a single camera could estimate the distance to each worker in a work zone. These days I’m also working with roadside video, following pairs of vehicles through the last few seconds before a crash.

## A few things I’ve worked on

- **[Work-Zone RGB-D Safety Dataset](https://github.com/WhiteMuyi/work-zone-safety-rgbd-dataset)** · We collected 1,685 images in simulated work zones and annotated 3,278 worker appearances so others can compare camera-based distance estimates with LiDAR measurements.
- **[Planar Calibration Tool](https://github.com/WhiteMuyi/planar-calibration-tool)** · If you know where a few points are on the road, this tool lets you click them in a photo or video frame and measure distances on the ground.
- **[Time-Lapse Cameras for Glacier Monitoring](https://github.com/WhiteMuyi/Timelapse-Photogrammetry-Wireless-Sensing-Network)** · An earlier project where we built a solar-powered camera system to photograph glaciers and track how they move.
